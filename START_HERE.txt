DHANUSHKKARAN S - PORTFOLIO WEBSITE
Updated 17 November 2026

GET STARTED
Extract the entire ZIP. Open index.html in your browser.
Keep the assets folder and all JS/CSS files beside index.html.
No build or installation is required. The design assets and resume work offline.
External profile/project links need an internet connection.

NEW CONTENT AND RESUME DOWNLOAD
The introduction, About, project summaries, skills, approach and contact copy
have been rewritten using your three reference portfolios for content structure.
All copy is original and uses your existing portfolio details.

Download resume links are in the hero, mobile menu and footer.
They download assets/Dhanushkkaran_S_Resume_Draft.pdf.
This is a clearly labelled draft, not an approved final resume. Unknown contact
details, full work history and school details have not been invented.

TO ADD YOUR FINAL RESUME
1. Put your approved PDF in assets/.
2. Edit resume.file, resume.label and resume.note in profile.js.
3. Also update the three data-resume-link href/download attributes in index.html
   for visitors who have JavaScript disabled.

PERSONAL DETAILS STILL NEEDED

Add these in profile.js. Footer email/phone links appear once values are filled.
